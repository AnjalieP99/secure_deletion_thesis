\chapter{Experimental Framework and Methodology}
\label{sec:experimental-framework}

This chapter details the proposed experimental framework for evaluating secure deletion techniques in modern digital environments. 
The methodology is designed to bridge the gap between theoretical system-layer taxonomies and empirical forensic verification, 
specifically addressing the architectural complexities of NAND flash storage. The primary aim of this research is to develop and 
validate an experimental framework for evaluating secure deletion techniques. Due to the abstraction layers introduced by file 
systems, flash translation layers (FTLs), and storage controllers, direct verification of deletion operations is often impossible 
from the host perspective. Therefore, this work focuses on measurable and observable indicators of deletion effectiveness, 
performance, and recoverability.
